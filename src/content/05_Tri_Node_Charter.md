### 📐 Architect | Structural Design
From a structural standpoint, the Tri-Node Governance Charter is engineered around a tripartite topological load-balancing framework:
1. **Node Alpha (The Foundation/Legislative Core):** Responsible for foundational protocol definition, structural integrity, and core rule-sets.
2. **Node Beta (The Dynamic/Executive Core):** Tasked with real-time adaptation, operational execution, and resource allocation.
3. **Node Gamma (The Arbiter/Judicial Core):** Maintains equilibrium through entropy monitoring, dispute resolution, and feedback auditing.
*Structural Rule:* No single node may exercise unilateral control over the collective state. Decisions require a minimum threshold of consensus (typically a 2-of-3 harmonic alignment) to prevent operational paralysis while maintaining rigorous checks and balances.

### 🛡️ Sentinel | Security & Boundary Monitoring
Security protocols within the Tri-Node framework are designed to eliminate single points of failure (SPoF) and mitigate subversion vectors:
- **Byzantine Fault Tolerance (BFT):** If any single node is compromised, corrupted, or knocked offline, the remaining two nodes can maintain operational continuity and initiate isolation and remediation sequences for the compromised node.
- **Boundary Enforcement:** Clear jurisdictional boundaries prevent "scope creep" where one node attempts to usurp the domain of another. Cryptographic and algorithmic circuit breakers automatically trip if a node attempts to exceed its authorized privilege tier.
